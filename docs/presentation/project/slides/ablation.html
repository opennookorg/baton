Each technique was built and measured on its own, against the same held-out set. Reweighting does most of the work. Baton is reweight plus shortcuts plus gap filling, chosen by forward selection on the learning stream, where a technique had to add at least 0.003 of stream accuracy to join. Merging and splitting lowered it, so they are out. The last two rows keep no graph at all. Naive Bayes ties Baton on the fixed test set, but on the stream, where each issue is routed before its answer is known, Baton is 5.5 points ahead.
