Each technique was built and measured on its own, against the same held-out set. Reweighting does most of the work. Baton is reweight plus shortcuts plus gap filling, chosen by forward selection on the learning stream, where a technique had to add at least 0.003 of stream accuracy to join. Merging and splitting did not clear that bar, so they are out, even though splitting has the best test top-3. Choosing by test score would be overfitting to the test set.
