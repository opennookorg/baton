The dashboard runs live at [baton-134858271117.us-central1.run.app](https://baton-134858271117.us-central1.run.app). It has four pages in the left sidebar. The theme button in the top right switches between light, dark and your system setting.

## The counters

Every dashboard page starts with the same five numbers:

| Counter | What it means |
|---|---|
| Issues learned | Fixed issues Baton has learned from. |
| Links reweighted | Times a link got stronger or weaker after an issue was fixed. |
| Names merged | Pairs of names Baton treats as the same thing (off in the shipped setup). |
| Shortcuts created | Direct "this word means this team" links Baton built from strong patterns. |
| Misroutes avoided | Issues per 1,000 that Baton sends to the right team and a plain counting router does not. |

## Learning curve

![The learning curve page](/images/page-curve.png)

This page shows how Baton got better as it learned. The 300 newest issues are kept aside as a test it never learns from. After every 100 issues learned, Baton is scored on that test.

- **Blue (Baton)** ends at 70%: the right team on its first guess, 7 times out of 10.
- **White (Count-only)** is the same graph without learning: 59%.
- **Dashed (Frozen)** is a graph built once and never updated, like an ownership list: 54%, flat forever.

Use **Top 1 / Top 3** to switch between "first guess right" and "right team in the top three". The buttons under the chart add one learning technique at a time, so you can see what each one adds.

## Try an issue

![Routing an issue](/images/page-try.png)

1. Click an example, or type a **Title**. The **Body** is optional: logs, file paths and error messages help.
2. Move the **Graph as of learning issue** slider to see what Baton would have said with less history: at 0 it knows nothing, at 1,700 it has learned everything.
3. Click **Route issue**.

On the right you get:

- **Confidence**: how sure Baton is about its top pick. Under 60%, a person (or an agent) should take a second look.
- **Entities known / unknown**: how many words and names from the issue Baton has seen before.
- **The top teams**, each with its share and the **path**, for example `watch → sig/api-machinery`. The path is the reason: this issue mentions "watch", and issues about "watch" are fixed by sig/api-machinery.

<Callout title="Try this">
  Paste `kube-scheduler: (Composite)PodGroup update event handlers don't distinguish a real update from a coalesced delete+create` and move the slider. At 500 Baton guesses sig/node (wrong). At 1,700 it says sig/scheduling (right), through `scheduler`.
</Callout>

## Ownership graph

![The ownership graph around kubelet](/images/page-explore.png)

This page draws what Baton has learned around one word or name. Type an entity (try `scheduler` or `kubelet`) and click **Show**.

- **White pills** are teams. **The blue dot** is the entity you picked. **Small grey dots** are past issues.
- **Thick green lines** are strong links. The number on a line is its strength.
- Drag to move things, scroll to zoom, hover to trace a link, click an entity to jump to it.
